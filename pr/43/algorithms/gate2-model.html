The Gate 2 model (E)

Sources:

  • src/tma2/gate2/: model.py and train.py (E-w), tables.py and es.py (E-s), predict.py, gate.py;
  • the CLIs tma2-dtables, tma2-g2train, tma2-g2predict and tma2-g2.

Requirements: SRD §5 (the Gate 2 row) and §7 step 4, Annex A §A.9, and spec 007.

E adds learning to the classical segmenter C (segmentation) in the two places where C decides. The physics layer still produces the estimate, with C's knots (a cap of 12). Both parts start at zero, so an untrained E is C. This is tested end to end: untrained E minus C scores 0 to 1e-9 in gap recovered.

E-w: weights on C's hand-over​

At each scored row k, C hands the physics layer a segmentation of [0, k]. That hand-over gives knots at the transitions, masked transition bearings and a grid start. E-w adds two things:

  • Per-bearing log-weights. For each row, weight_bound · tanh(head) is added to C's log-weights, so a masked bearing stays masked.
  • A start correction at row k: Δ log range, Δ course and Δ log speed applied to C's grid start. The correction is applied as a difference from the zero correction, so zero heads give C's start bit for bit.

The encoder is Gate 1's causal transformer (Gate 1 model) with one extra input channel: C's code at prefix k (steady 0, transition 1, unknown 0.5, and 0 after k). Inputs after row k are zeroed and attention is causal, and the physics layer masks rows after k, so nothing after the prefix reaches the estimate (tested by perturbing every later row).

Training. The loss is the CRB-normalised squared error of (log range, vx, vy) at the row, for each prefix:

  • each term is capped at 100;
  • prefixes whose solve failed (cost above 20 × their bearings) are excluded;
  • an auxiliary term trains the start correction, because implicit differentiation gives the start no gradient;
  • early stopping uses the trimmed mean of the validation loss.

E-w trains on C's segmentation of the training stream.

E-s: evidence on C's confirmation​

C accepts a nominated transition when its cost drop Δ beats a χ² threshold τ′ (τ = 13.8 for a transition that has ended, 4 degrees of freedom; see segmentation). E-s is spec 005's causal evidence network. It emits e per row, and e > 0 means steady. The evidence shifts the threshold by s = clamp(2 Σ e, ± τ/2) over the candidate's rows. C's own nominations are kept: E-s acts on decisions, not on candidates.

Decision tables.

  • tma2-dtables re-runs C and records every decision: row a, start, length, Δ, τ′, accepted.
  • A decision within τ/2 of its threshold can be flipped by evidence. For each such decision, the table holds the bound-normalised error at every later scored row twice: once with the transition and once without it, keeping C's other decisions as C made them.
  • Decisions further than τ/2 from their threshold cannot flip, so their rows are constants of the loss.

Relaxed acceptance.

  • The loss is the mean over decision rows of p·e_with + (1 − p)·e_without, where p = σ((Δ − τ′ − s)/T) and T is a declared temperature.
  • At e = 0 and T → 0 this is C's error.
  • Validation uses the hard rule (accept if Δ − τ′ − s > 0), which is what inference does.

Limits of the relaxation. It takes one decision at a time:

  • a decision's effect on later decisions is ignored;
  • it holds C's best candidate fixed, while at inference the evidence can also move the best start or length.

E = E-s, then E-w​

At inference, E-s's evidence goes into C's confirmation, and E-w then weights the hand-over of the resulting segmentation. E-w is trained on C's segmentation rather than E-s's. Training it on E-s's would cost a full re-segmentation of the training stream per E-s seed, and E-s changes only near-threshold decisions. E-w alone, on C's segmentation, is reported as an ablation.

What E learned, and the classical controls​

On Gate 2's records E-s's evidence sits almost entirely within a few rows of the ownship zig: it learned not to accept a target transition at the ownship's own manoeuvre (a zig veto). E-w's log-weights are mostly a recency profile (a fading memory). Two rules with nothing learned stand in for them (gate2.predict, model kind veto in tma2-g2predict):

  • C′: C's confirmation with fixed evidence of 1e3 on rows zig − W … zig + W (W = 6), so any candidate whose evidence rows touch the window gets the largest shift, +τ/2, and every other decision is C's exactly. A threshold of 1.5τ there, not a ban.
  • fade: clip(a − b·age, ±2) added to C's log-weights, age = k − row (a = 0.73, b = 0.0079, read off E-w's mean profile); masked bearings stay masked.
  • C″: both.

On Gate 2's test seed C″ recovers +0.018 of E's +0.025 over C; E − C″ is +0.007 [+0.001, +0.012] (experiments/20261107-g2-veto/). Later gates keep C″ as a standing control.

Export​

E exports as two ONNX graphs (constitution VI, three runtimes to 1e-5 in CI): E-s's evidence network (segment.evidence_export --plan configs/gate2/g2-a.json) and E-w on C's hand-over of one prefix (gate2.export: the encoder, heads and 12-knot physics layer, its 100 iterations one ONNX Loop). C's nomination and confirmation stay host code between them.

The gate​

tma2-g2 scores E's prediction files beside C, one leg, the oracle, GLR + confirm, the frozen IMM and EKF, and naive, on the same rows. Every physics-layer solve uses the 12-knot cap.

CheckMeasureRule
G2-aE / IMM, median range and course, all G5 rowswholly below 1
G2-bE / one leg, range, course and speed, settled rowswholly below 1.1
G2-cE's gap recovered (median range, one leg to oracle)wholly above 0.5
G2-dE − C in gap recoveredwholly above 0; void if the shuffled control beats C

Each interval is a scenario-clustered paired bootstrap: 97.5%, 2,000 resamples, inverted_cdf medians.
