Two stages, chosen separately

  • Splitters: none · told the truth · Debrief 2015 arctan slicing · independent slices · joined legs · C
  • A legs panel (found, missed, false, timing) and "Run all splitters"

The arctan law, three ways

  • While both hold course: tan b = (A + Bt)/(C + Dt). A manoeuvre breaks it.
  • Debrief 2015: per steady ownship leg, best single split, 2-min buffer, accept if score < whole × ZigRatio. Blind to a turn made during ownship's turn.
  • Independent slices: ownship subtracted, recursive. Each slice fits its own leg, so a slice without an ownship turn absorbs a misplaced break: the split's drop is flat over 15 min.
  • Joined legs: position continuous, velocity steps at knots, every parameter refitted; knots added one at a time, then re-placed with the others held. "The window to notice, the whole history to decide" (note 07).

Not achieved

  • No result: three presets, five draws, thresholds set on the same presets. No false-alarm rate, no interval.
  • C is slow in the browser (about 3½ min per two-hour window) and here poor: it has no zig veto, so ownship's turns become false transitions.
  • The standing time-late system ctl (arc model, prior, evidence) is not in the browser.
  • Joined legs use instant knots; a turn inside ownship's turn is placed late.

Next (owner's choice)

  • Score joined legs on Annex B data against C″ and ctl on spent seeds (development), then register if it holds.
  • Give joined legs a turn of finite length (the arc model's) where a knot meets ownship's turn.
  • Or leave it as a teaching tool: the page now shows why leg finding, not the solve, is the hard part.
