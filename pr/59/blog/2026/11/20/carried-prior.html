A carried prior across gaps: it helps where a period cannot answer alone

November 20, 2026 · 7 min read

Spec 027 showed that carrying a start from one period to the next does not substitute for one solve through the gaps. This registered study carried information instead. The previous period's declared state and its uncertainty become a prior on the next period's starting state. By the rule registered beforehand, the carried prior helps against solving each period alone on Annex B targets, and no harm was shown. The review panel approved and narrowed the result:

  • the gain is in periods the bearings leave undetermined;
  • the 4× inflation chosen in development is not shown to matter;
  • the carried uncertainty covers the truth because it is too wide.

The joint solve stays the standing system. Nothing was trained. The illustrated companion is the slides post.

What was built​

For each period after an engagement's first, the evidence of the standing control ctl gains a Gaussian prior on the period's starting state (position, course, speed). That prior is built in three steps:

  1. Take the previous period's declared track at its last row.
  2. Take that state's Laplace covariance from the previous period's posterior fit.
  3. Run both through the gap at constant velocity. Annex B's manoeuvre rate and sizes are added as process noise.

The prior replaces the standing prior's terms for those four parameters (range log-uniform, bearing and course uniform, a soft speed band), and the period's search starts from the carried state. Everything else in ctl is unchanged.

How k4 was chosen​

Development used spent seeds only.

  • Seed 361. The uninflated prior was overconfident: the truth sat inside its 95% region in about half of periods. On this seed it harmed Annex B periods that had an ownship turn of their own (0.51).
  • The choice. Five variants were run: inflation by 2 or 4 in sd, and mixtures with the standing prior. A rule written before the results kept only variants with no own-turn harm and coverage of at least 0.80. It chose k4, the covariance 4 times wider in sd.
  • Seed 360. A declared check passed here.

How it was decided​

All comparisons use periods after an engagement's first, on spec 025's rows. Each is a ratio of period median range error, with a 95% interval from resampling engagements.

  • Helps: ctl alone ÷ k4 in range is wholly above 1 on Annex B, the split that decides.
  • Harms: wholly below 1 in range, course or speed on any split, or in range on Annex B periods with their own ownship turn.

Validation used seed 362, where soundness passed. The test used seed 363, scored once.

The result​

splitalone ÷ k4, range (median per period)k4 ÷ joint, range
Annex B (decides)1.59 [1.21, 2.13] (1.09): helps1.40 [0.98, 2.01]
busier targets1.60 [1.28, 1.99] (1.16)1.25 [0.94, 1.66]
2% wild bearings1.35 [1.01, 1.87] (1.01)1.74 [1.23, 2.43]
held course2.32 [1.49, 3.62] (1.27)5.20 [2.93, 9.00]

No harm on any split in any quantity. On Annex B periods with their own turn the ratio is 1.22 [0.84, 1.85]. On Annex B, k4 is better in 57% of periods and worse in 32%. It is twice as good in 32% and twice as bad in 11%.

What it means, as the panel narrowed it​

  • The gain is where the bearings leave a period undetermined.
    • Annex B periods without their own ownship turn: 1.97. With one: 1.22.
    • On rows a period determines by itself: 1.04 [0.77, 1.48], level.
    • So a carried prior makes undetermined periods answerable. It does not improve a period's own estimate. This is the same pattern as spec 025's joint solve.
  • The inflation is not shown to matter.
    • The uninflated prior does as well on the test seed: uninflated ÷ k4 is 0.99 [0.78, 1.25].
    • The uninflated prior would also have passed the rule.
    • The own-turn harm that motivated k4 appeared on one development seed only.
    • What is supported is "a carried prior helps", not "inflate by 4".
  • k4's uncertainty is too wide, not calibrated.
    • It covers the truth (0.92 to 0.98) because its spread is about 2.6 times too wide: median squared distance 0.51 against 3.36 for a calibrated prior.
    • The process noise is mis-shaped. It puts half of a turn's change into speed, and constant velocity runs the mean ahead of a turning target.
    • A single history's covariance is too narrow in the tail even with no manoeuvre in the gap.
    • After inflation the prior carries little beyond a band on position.
  • Against the alternatives.
    • Against spec 027's carried start (not registered): 1.60 [1.05, 2.74] on Annex B.
    • Against joining: on the line on Annex B (lower limit 0.97 to 1.02 by bootstrap seed). Separated with wild bearings and on held course.
    • Joining is not shown unnecessary anywhere.
  • Across seeds. Annex B alone ÷ k4 was 1.59, 1.22, 1.51 and 1.59 on seeds 360 to 363. The one that would not pass (361) is the seed k4 was chosen on.
  • When it applies.
    • Within this simulator, when only the previous declared state and its covariance are kept, a period solved under them is better than one solved from nothing.
    • Time-late, the earlier bearings are normally kept, and then the joint solve is the answer.
    • A real hand-over was not tested: a track from another estimator, with uncertain association.
  • Secondary splits are reported, not decided.
    • Wild bearings is on the line and carried by a tail (median 1.01).
    • Held course's 2.32 is measured against a baseline that is very poor there.

Forecasts​

Right:

  • helps on Annex B (1.59, against a forecast of 1.2 to 1.6), with one development seed in two failing;
  • busier about 1.55 (1.60), held course larger, no harm.

Wrong:

  • k4 ÷ joint on Annex B (forecast about 2.1; 1.40, on the line).
  • Coverage was "right" (0.92 to 0.98), but by being too wide.

What was not done​

  • A calibrated carried uncertainty: a scale near 1.5, noise shaped to turns and speed changes, or a prior over the previous period's candidate histories.
  • A registered comparison of k4 with the uninflated prior and with the carried start.
  • Code that applies the rule: the verdict was applied by hand.
  • IMM and EKF tracks.

Recorded, not repaired (the code is registered):

  • the wrapped course Gaussian is normalised on the line, not the circle;
  • a speed shoulder is left in the fit;
  • three small fragilities.

What next (the owner's choice)​

The panel's order:

  1. A consistency flag. Compare the carried prior's distance with the period's own fit, and test whether it finds the twice-as-bad periods.
  2. A calibrated carried prior on spent seeds. Scale, shaped noise, the manoeuvre mixture and candidate histories.
  3. A stress split with longer gaps and more periods, where a carried state is the realistic option.
  4. A real hand-over. Only if that case matters: a carried state from another estimator, with uncertain association.

This study was developed and registered as spec 028 and renumbered 030 after another session's specs 028 and 029 merged first; its registered seeds 362 and 363 had also been claimed by spec 029 in the long-engagement simulator (different engagements; recorded in the decision record).
