Slides: time-late over several periods of one target, joined through the gaps

November 18, 2026 · 2 min read

The illustrated companion to the spec 025 write-up: one engagement as tracks, the registered verdict, where the gain comes from, manoeuvres in the gaps, and what the review panel narrowed.

What the figures show​

  • Track: one Annex B engagement of the test seed (353), chosen by a rule in the script (the median engagement among the stored three-period ones): truth red, ownship blue, ctl alone per period orange, the joint solve green and dashed through the gaps.
  • Verdict: ctl alone ÷ joint in range on every split with 95% engagement intervals, on the registered rows and on rows the period determines alone.
  • Kinds: the same for periods with and without an ownship turn of their own.
  • Gaps: true manoeuvres inside gaps found and missed, and the share of quiet gaps with an invented manoeuvre.

Reproducing the figures​

uv run python scripts/research/tl8_charts.py --record experiments/20261118-tl8 --out-dir site/static/img/2026-11-18-joined-periods-slides

The script reads only the committed record (results branch results/timelate-tl-k-test-44252585727); each figure's .json sidecar names the inputs' digests and the commit. Data: seed 353, 5-second bearings, configs/timelate/tl-joined-*.json, generated in the CI run.
