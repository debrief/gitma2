Several periods of one target across long gaps

2026-11-21 · spec 033 · gitma2 · development study, spent seed · nothing trained

The question

  • With gaps of 5–30 min, one solve through the gaps beat each period alone (spec 025: 3.10 on Annex B)
  • Here: 6-hour engagements from the long simulator, 2–4 periods of 15–30 min, gaps of 30–90 min
  • Two runs on the same targets and layouts: the ownship turning every 20–40 min, or holding course 1–2 h
  • Compared: ctl alone, the joint solve, and the carried prior k4 (spec 030)

By gap length: one band separated, no trend shown

Holding course: 1.34, 2.85 [1.49, 6.05], 0.71 [0.47, 1.02]. Turning: 1.40, 1.27, 0.80. Only 50–70 min (holding) is separated; beyond 70 min both read below 1, not separated (medians 0.95, 0.88). Bands chosen after the data. The clearest signal is a period without its own ownship turn: about 2 below 70 min in both runs.

Holding course doubles everyone's error

Periods without their own ownship turn are poorly determined whatever the method; joining recovers part. k4 (as tuned in spec 030) is level with alone in both runs (0.94, 1.05).

Beyond an hour: the gap history is not chosen well

The true gap structure would still help (2.35, 2.48 against alone). The Laplace evidence rarely separates the true gap history from a simpler one (13 of 21 within ±10 units; 123 declared against 178 true). Whether better search would help is not resolved: the re-run declared other histories in 5 engagements.

Not achieved

  • No registered result: one spent seed, 40 engagements, about 20–30 periods per gap band, bands chosen after the data
  • No trend with gap length is separated; beyond 70 min nothing is shown either way
  • No joint solve that breaks at long gaps was built; the long simulator's ownship steers on the true target

What follows

  • A suggestion, not a rule: join a period without its own ownship turn across gaps up to about an hour
  • Owner's choice: stop; register a gap × own-turn rule on fresh seeds; or first check the evidence on the scored run's own histories
