The time-late track as a whole

What six studies established, and what they did not

15 November 2026 · specs 013 to 019 · gitma2

The question

  • After the fact: the target's whole track over a period of 20 to 120 minutes, and when it manoeuvred.
  • Every 5-second bearing of the period is available.
  • Six studies in five days, each registered before its fresh seed and decided by a panel of five.

What is standing

What it gained over the old system

2.1 to 2.4 on Annex B targets, 1.6 to 1.7 on busier targets, about 5 with wild bearings, level on held course.

What remains

Undeclared control, reference with free times: 1.3 on Annex B targets, 1.8 to 2.1 on busier ones. Declared control, times held: 1.6 and 2.3 to 2.7. References, not bounds.

The attempts to beat it

A learned model's read-out, a prior chosen per period and a hedged track did not. The true statistics are worth a tenth to a sixth on busier targets: two heavy tails that nearly cancel.

The last diagnostic

130 used periods, nothing separated. The gain builds along the ladder, legs first; the other parts alone do nothing. A search of its own did no better than a refit (1.03 both).

Three periods

Chosen by rule from the stored periods of spec 017's test. The worst case is shown as plainly as the best.

Corrected along the way

  • One reading withdrawn; one gate stopped at validation and re-registered.
  • The evidence wrong five times; the last unrepaired.
  • Four forecasts wrong; three rules that measured the wrong thing; one statistic that read spread as a tail.
  • "Ceiling", "bound" and "limit" taken back for fits told the truth.

What was not achieved, and what comes next

  • Learning: not shown, at a small budget, with one direct model and one untuned read-out.
  • Never tested: a learned proposer inside the search; several periods of one target; ownship advice; real data.
  • The time-late track stops here unless the owner reopens it.
