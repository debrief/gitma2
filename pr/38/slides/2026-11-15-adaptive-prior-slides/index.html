What was not achieved

  • Any gain on busier targets; my forecast of a trade was wrong.
  • A family that contained Annex B's own prior: the cost there is the design's.
  • A spread fit to show: honest where candidates disagree, too narrow where the mixture is sure and wrong.
  • A search under each prior; tests of the runner; IMM and EKF on these periods.
