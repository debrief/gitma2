Slides: Gate T, the track after the fact

November 12, 2026 · 2 min read

The illustrated companion to the Gate T write-up: the question (the whole track over a period, after the fact), why a test of fit cannot answer it, the classical search that uses the owner's manoeuvre statistics, the gate on test seed 331, and what the review panel narrowed.

What the figures show​

  • Two histories, the same bearings. One tuning period in which a history with no manoeuvre fits the bearings as well as the true one.
  • Told the manoeuvres, the arc model reaches the bound. Tuning seed 329: the chord solve and the arc solve against the Cramér–Rao bound.
  • The ratio on three seeds. C″ ÷ search with its 95% interval, on Annex B data and the shifted prior, beside the registered thresholds.
  • Every system as a track. Median period range error on the test seed.
  • The reference by how tight the bound is. The fit from neutral starts against the fit started at the truth, which sits below the bound where the bound is loose.
  • Stated probability against hit rate, and manoeuvres found minus true.

There are no track plots with uncertainty ellipses: the search states candidate tracks with probabilities and no covariance.

Reproducing the figures​

uv run python scripts/research/gt_charts.py --out-dir site/static/img/2026-11-12-gate-t-slides
uv run python scripts/research/tl_charts.py --out-dir site/static/img/2026-11-12-time-late-slides --data <dir>

The first script reads only committed records (experiments/20261112-gt-val/, 20261112-gt2-val/, 20261112-gt2/); the second made the first two figures from tuning records and, for the ambiguity figure, from tuning seed 329 (count 400, configs/timelate/tl-ranges.json). Each figure's .json sidecar names the inputs' digests and the commit. The gate's data are seeds 330 and 332 (validation) and 331 (test), 5-second bearings, configs/timelate/tl-ranges.json and tl-shift-ranges.json, generated in the scoring runs.
