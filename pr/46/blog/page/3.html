November 13, 2026 · 7 min read

It also found the true-history reference drifting into other histories. The owner chose to repair and re-register. Spec 015 fixed the three, decided the same five quantities on fresh seeds (336 validation, 337 test) and trained nothing. The registered figures reproduce, and the registered rule again says no GPU budget. The panel approved with findings, and its main one is that the headroom figure now depends most on the thing we repaired last: how the reference is fitted. The scaled regressor: a learned control that beat the classical system, and what that does not show

November 12, 2026 · 7 min read

Spec 012 trained a direct sequence-to-state model on about twenty million simulated engagements, on a rented GPU, as a control: what does a learned model with no physics layer and no segmenter reach, and what does it not? On a fresh test seed it is partial against the bound on a steady target and it beats the standing classical system after target manoeuvres. The second result is the first time in this project that something learned has clearly beaten the classical line. The review panel accepted the measurements and rejected most of what was first said about them. This post reports both.
