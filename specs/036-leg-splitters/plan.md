## Design

1. `arctan.js`: the three-parameter arctan law (LM), ownship legs, Debrief slicing; the
   ownship-subtracted fit (gitma's LM from `lsq.js`), multi-leg independent slicing (recursive,
   continuation warm starts, a joint re-placement pass); joined legs (`fitKnots`, a
   Jacobi-free LM over 4 + 2K parameters; forward selection and re-placement).
2. `splitters.js`: plug-ins (info, `split(problem, ctx)`) and C's pipeline without E's evidence.
3. `solvers.js`: `layer` takes `problem.knots` from the splitter (four strongest by `stat` when
   more); `layer-cv`/`layer-oracle` kept callable.
4. `worker.js`: split then solve; `compare` message.
5. `demo.js`: drop-downs, legs panel, marks, diamonds, comparison panel; `normaliseScenario`.
6. Tests and the figure script `scripts/research/demo_splitters.mjs`.

## Research

- R1: independent slices are too flexible. On the two-turn preset the drop in cost for a split
  is flat from 65 to 83 min (about 10,000 σ² throughout): a short slice without an ownship turn
  fits any break through its free range. Joining the legs removes the freedom.
- R2: thresholds. Multi τ = 40 σ² and joined τ = 25 σ² were set on held-course presets (good
  geometry, endfire, no zig) at σ = 1° and 2°, 8 draws: no false split for any variant. A small
  check on the page's toy scenarios, not a false-alarm rate.
- R3: run time in Node (one core): Debrief 0.3–2 s, independent slices 2–10 s, joined legs 0.4–8 s,
  C about 220 s on a two-hour window (its leg search about 40 s, then 109 confirmations).
