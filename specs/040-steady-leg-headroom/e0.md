# E0: two facts about the existing solve (read from the code, 28 November 2026)

**1. Does the told-truth solve bound target speed and initial range?** No.
- `physics/layer.py` imports `MAX_RANGE_M` and `MAX_SPEED_MPS` only to set the `plausible` flag
  on a finished solve; they do not constrain it.
- The start is a coarse grid (12 ranges from 1 to 40 km along the first bearing, 8 courses,
  speeds 2, 5 and 9 m/s); after that the solve is free.
- The only prior in the told-truth solve is a speed-continuity weight across a course-only
  change (`physics/segmentation.py`, `oracle_inputs(..., speed_prior=True)`), as
  `segment.score.oracle` calls it.
- Spec 039 adds a read-out told the generator's range distribution and speed support
  (`headroom/profile.py`); it is a separate system, not part of the told-truth solve.

**2. Does it exclude transition bearings, model the turn as an arc, or both?** Neither.
- A true change becomes two hard knots, at its start and at its end, where the target's velocity
  may step: the chord approximation (`docs/research/05`). The bearings inside the change keep
  full weight and are fitted by the chord. A manoeuvre therefore has four free unknowns.
- `mask_turns` (zero weight inside each change) exists as a diagnostic and is off.
- The arc model (a turn at a constant rate, a speed change at a constant rate) exists only in
  the time-late code (`timelate/arc.py`).
- With C″, the knots are C's own declared transition start and end
  (`gate2/inputs.prefix_inputs`): the transition length is C's estimate, not fixed. A transition
  still open at the scored row is handed over by the convention `knot_at_k`.

**Consequence for E3.** Because a chord has free velocity on both sides and inside the change,
it can absorb some error in knot timing; the stand-alone solve's sensitivity to timing (H3) may
not carry over. Because the solve is unbounded, T-bounds is a real variant, not a skip.

**Cost units (the plan's risk 2).** The plain layer's cost is the sum of squared bearing
residuals over σ², so "25 σ²" is 25 in its units. The robust solve whitens with a fitted error
model and Huber weights, so its cost is not in those units; thresholds under mismatch must be
stated in the whitened χ² and the conversion recorded.
