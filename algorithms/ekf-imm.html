Tracking filters: EKF and IMM

Source: src/tma2/references/ekf.py, imm.py. Line-for-line ports of gitma's trackers (packages/classical/src/ekf.ts, imm.ts), including the finite-difference Jacobians, so that the frozen phase-4 reference imm-cv0.01-ct0.03-w0.01-s0.001-p4-r8 gives the same rows in both repositories (checked on the committed fixtures).

Common ground​

  • Start where classical does: initialised at zig_index from the batch least-squares solution on the samples so far, with covariance σ²(JᵀJ)⁻¹ (× p0_scale) propagated to that time.
  • Run causally: one update per bearing; one row per sample from zig_index + 1, the same rows as the classical per-timestep mode.
  • Wrapped innovation and Joseph-form covariance update; measurement variance = the scenario's σ² (floored at 10⁻⁴ rad) × r_scale.
  • Divergence: a state outside the plausibility bounds is reported non-converged and the filter re-initialises from the batch solution, at once and then every 6 samples until it succeeds.

EKF (constant velocity)​

State (x, y, vₓ, v_y); white-noise-acceleration process noise σ_a: Q = σ_a² [τ⁴/4, τ³/2; τ³/2, τ²] per axis. Implausible rows keep the state in the row (gitma's choice) but are flagged.

IMM (constant velocity + coordinated turn)​

State (x, y, vₓ, v_y, ω). Two EKFs: CV (ω carried, unused; σ_cv) and CT (closed-form coordinated-turn motion with small-angle limits; σ_ct and a turn-rate random walk σ_ω). Standard Blom–Bar-Shalom cycle: mix with switch probability min(0.5, s·τ), predict (Jacobians by central differences: 10⁻⁴·max(1, |x|) for position and velocity, 10⁻⁷ for ω), update both models, likelihoods exp(−ν²/2S)/√(2πS) (falling back to the predicted probabilities if their sum is 0 or non-finite), combine as the mixture mean. Initial μ = (0.9, 0.1), ω prior sd 0.05 rad/s. The normalised innovation squared (NIS) is recorded for consistency checks.

In gitma2​

The IMM, re-tuned on gitma2's validation split with declared grids before any training, is the reference for manoeuvring targets at Gate 2 and in the steady-leg detector's downstream checks (Annex A). The EKF is reported beside it.
