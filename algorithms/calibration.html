Uncertainty calibration (spec 008)

Sources:

  • src/tma2/physics/uncertainty.py (the covariance a row states);
  • src/tma2/eval/calibration.py (the measures);
  • src/tma2/calib/ (head, loss, training, temperature, calibrated files, Gate 3's criteria);
  • the CLIs tma2-g3train, tma2-g3predict and tma2-g3.

Requirements: SRD §7 step 5 (first item) and spec 008.

What a row states​

The physics layer's damped solve ends with a Gauss–Newton covariance of the state at the scored row, P = σ²(JᵀJ)⁻¹ in (x, y, vx, vy). Every prediction row now carries it: the ten entries of its upper triangle (cov_xyvv), a usable flag (cov_ok: finite, symmetric, positive definite) and three standard deviations derived by the delta method:

  • log range, from the gradient of ½ log((x − ox)² + (y − oy)²);
  • course, from the gradient of atan2(vx, vy);
  • speed, from the gradient of √(vx² + vy²).

C, E, the classical controls, the IMM and the EKF (its 4×4 block; for the IMM, the mixed estimate's) all write the same fields. Points are unchanged.

E-w and the fading memory rescale the bearings' weights, so their P is not on C's scale until it is calibrated. This is why the calibration is measured per estimator and not assumed.

Measures​

Each row is scored against the truth state at that row. All intervals are the scorer's scenario-clustered bootstrap (97.5%, 2,000 resamples).

The interval score rewards calibration and sharpness together, so it is the score of record for learning (G3-c): an estimator cannot win it by stating huge intervals.

The calibration head​

A small network reads E's encoder state at the row, the log raw sds and C's code at the row, and outputs one scale s = 2·tanh(·). The calibrated covariance is e^(2s)·P. The state is never changed (FR-020), so Gate 2's results hold for the calibrated E by construction.

The output layer starts at zero, so the untrained head is the identity.

The loss is β-NLL (β = 0.5): ½e^(−2s)q + 4s, with q = eᵀP⁻¹e the raw NEES, each row weighted by the stop-gradient of e^(2sβ) and q capped at 100. Training uses E's frozen features cached over stream seed 318; early stopping uses validation ordinals 0–499. Three seeds and a shuffled-label control are trained. The shuffled control is scored at its best and its last epoch.

The classical control​

A per-slice temperature: one scale per cell of (minutes since the zig × open at k), fitted by the same loss on validation ordinals 0–499. It is what a careful engineer would do without learning, so the head must beat it (G3-c) before any calibration is credited to learning.

Gate 3​

The criteria are fixed by the SRD (§5) and the decision record before any training result:

CriterionTest
G3-a95% coverage of log range, course and speed: interval wholly in [0.92, 0.98]
G3-bshare of rows with NEES within the χ²₄ 95% point (missing rows outside): interval wholly in [0.92, 0.98] (owner, 2026-11-08)
G3-chead − temperature, mean interval score in log range: wholly below 0; void if the shuffled control also beats the temperature on validation
G3-dthe calibrated E's point errors equal E's on every row

tma2-g3 reads a finished scoring run's stored arrays (errors.npz), so nothing is re-solved and the verdict does not depend on the CPU family.
