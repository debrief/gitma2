title: "Several periods of one target across long gaps: join up to about an hour"

Spec 025 found that one solve through the gaps beats solving each period of a target alone,
with gaps of 5 to 30 minutes. This development study stretched the gaps to 30–90 minutes. It used
two runs on one spent seed: one where the ownship turns often, and one where it holds course.
Joining still helps up to about an hour, especially when the ownship holds course. Beyond about
70 minutes it reverses in both runs. A diagnostic shows why: across an hour of unseen bearings,
the evidence prefers a simpler gap history than the one that happened. The carried prior of spec
030 does nothing across gaps this long. Nothing was trained and nothing here is a registered
result. The illustrated companion is [the slides post](/blog/2026/11/21/long-gaps-slides).

## What was run

- **Engagements:** 40 six-hour engagements from the long simulator (specs 023–026). Targets
  follow Annex B's statistics. There is no realism layer.
- **Layout:** each engagement is cut into 2 to 4 periods of 15–30 minutes, with gaps of 30–90
  minutes.
- **Two runs on the same engagements, targets and layouts:**
  - the ownship turns every 20–40 minutes, so most periods hold their own turn;
  - the ownship holds each leg 1–2 hours, so most periods do not.
- **Systems,** unchanged from spec 030:
  - `ctl` alone on each period;
  - the joint solve over the span, with the gaps' bearings masked;
  - the carried prior k4.
- **Seed:** spent seed 348 (spec 023's).
- **Diagnostic:** on the 21 engagements with a gap of at least 70 minutes, the evidence of the
  joint solve's declared history was compared with the true history fitted the same way.

## What it found

| | turning every 20–40 min | holding 1–2 h |
|---|---|---|
| later periods with their own turn | 0.78 | 0.23 |
| median error: alone / joint / k4 | 0.117 / 0.121 / 0.112 | 0.273 / 0.174 / 0.188 |
| alone ÷ joint | 1.20 [0.90, 1.60] | 1.34 [1.01, 1.84] |
| … gap 30–50 / 50–70 / 70–90 min | 1.40 / 1.27 / 0.80 | 1.34 / **2.85** / 0.71 |
| alone ÷ k4 | 0.94 | 1.05 |

- **Holding course roughly doubles everyone's error.** Joining recovers part of that loss.
  Where the ownship turns often, each period mostly answers for itself and joining adds little.
- **Beyond about 70 minutes joining loses in both runs.** The two runs share their targets and
  gaps, so this is one observation seen twice, not a replication. Only one gap band is separated
  from 1 (holding course, 50–70 minutes).
- **The carried prior is level with `ctl` alone.** After an hour of possible manoeuvres its
  stated uncertainty covers almost everything (covers the truth 0.99 of the time, median d² 0.82),
  so it carries almost no information.
- **Cost.** The joint solve takes about 11 to 16 times as long as `ctl` alone (a median of 625 to
  741 s an engagement), and it rarely reaches the cap of ten manoeuvres.

## Why joining fails beyond about an hour

- **The evidence prefers the simpler history.** In 15 of the 21 engagements with a gap of at
  least 70 minutes, the declared history has at least the true history's evidence. The joint
  solve declares 123 manoeuvres against 178 that happened.
- **Why it does.** The truth fits the bearings slightly better (median +1.6), but its extra
  legs and changes cost more prior (about −29) than its Occam volume gives back (+22). With the
  gap's bearings masked, nothing asks for the manoeuvres that happened there.
- **The search falls short in only 6.** Joining loses either way: 0.80 where the evidence is at
  fault, 0.57 where the search is.
- **Not every engagement.** Some engagements where the evidence prefers the declared history
  still gain from joining.

This is identifiability, not search. A better search would not make joining win beyond an hour.

## What it supports, and what not

- **Suggested practice:** join periods across gaps up to about an hour, and solve each period
  alone beyond that. It is suggested by the band trend and explained by the diagnostic; it is not
  decided.
- **Limits:**
  - one spent seed and 40 engagements, with 20 to 30 periods per band;
  - the threshold is read from three bands;
  - the long simulator's ownship steers on the true target;
  - C″'s start reads only the first ownship turn;
  - the Laplace evidence throughout;
  - the true history is fitted with the prior, not told.
- **Edge effect:** an earlier version of the diagnostic was misled by a manoeuvre cut by the
  span's start, which cost about 470 units of prior. Such manoeuvres are now left out (the edge
  rule of specs 014 and 015).
