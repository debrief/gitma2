The owner's hypothesis: knowledge of the previous track can usefully constrain the solution for
the next leg. [Spec 031](/blog/2026/11/20/held-comparators) found that with ownship legs of 1–2 h,
the standing 2-hour window always contains a turn, so the prior had nothing to rescue. This study
makes the ownship hold each leg for 2–4 h, so some standing 2-hour windows are blind.

**The result:** on blind 2-hour windows, `ctl` ÷ `ctl-prior` is **1.54 [1.16, 2.03]**, and the
registered rule reads **"ctl-prior better"**. Against a joint solve over the same past bearings it
is **1.11 [0.84, 1.45], not separated**. The prior does no harm on windows with one turn; it helps
there too, at 1.26.

Spec: `specs/032-long-legs/`. Record: `experiments/20261120-legs/`.

<!-- truncate -->

## The design

- **Engagements:** spec 024's realism, with ownship legs of 2–4 h, contact 1, and 6- and 12-hour
  engagements.
- **Windows:** the standing configuration, 2-hour windows overlapping 30 min.
- **Systems on blind (turn-free) windows:**
  - `ctl`, the window solved alone;
  - `ctl-prior`, which carries the previous window's state and covariance as a prior, with spec
    028's guard;
  - a joint solve: `ctl` on the window and the one before it, 3.5 h of past bearings.
- **Estimands:**
  - **E1** = `ctl` ÷ `ctl-prior`; **E2** = joint ÷ `ctl-prior`.
  - **Harm** is read on one-turn windows.
  - Each is a geometric mean of per-window median range error, with a bootstrap over engagements.
- **Registration:** before seeds 367 and 368 existed. The test was raised to 50 + 50 engagements
  on tuning's yield (`experiments/20261120-legs-decision/`).

## What happened

Test: seed 368, 100 engagements, scored once. Soundness passed.

| reading | range | median | `ctl-prior` better | twice as good / twice as bad |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **E1** `ctl` ÷ `ctl-prior` | **1.537 [1.156, 2.029]** | 1.25 | 59% | 38% / 18% |
| **E2** joint ÷ `ctl-prior` | 1.113 [0.840, 1.445] | 1.24 | 61% | 24% / 19% |
| harm (one-turn windows) | 1.259 [1.056, 1.500] | 1.21 | 60% | 31% / 16% |

- **Median range error on decided blind windows:**

  | system | median error |
  |---|---|
  | `ctl` | 16.7% |
  | joint | 12.8% |
  | `ctl-prior` | 10.4% |
  | reference told the true history | 3.3% |

- **The prior does the work, not the carried start:** `ctl-carry` ÷ `ctl-prior` is 1.52.
- **The forecasts came true:** E1 "better" (probability 0.45), E2 "not separated" (0.5), no harm
  (0.95).

## What the panel narrowed

Three reviewers each approved with findings (`experiments/20261120-legs/review.md`). Every decided
number was reproduced independently.

- **Half the blind windows are outside the claim.**
  - Holding a 2–4 h leg opens the range, often beyond detection. 80 of 165 blind windows were
    lost to contact (median 72 km) and are unscored.
  - The claim covers the 85 where contact held: median 37 km, about half the bearings detected.
- **It is a far-range effect.**
  - E1 is about 2.2 beyond 35 km and about 1.0 under; at 15–25 km it is 0.82.
  - Range and contact loss move together here and cannot be separated.
- **E1 is "more data beats no data".** E2 is the fair comparison, and the compact hand-over is
  not shown to lose to keeping the bearings, nor to beat them.
- **The prior comes from a window that saw a turn** in 83 of 85 cases. Chains of blind windows are
  untested.
- **Seeds move.**
  - E1 was 1.32, 0.87 and 1.54 across the three seeds; pooled, it is 1.37 [1.05, 1.80].
  - The joint solve against `ctl` flipped between seeds; pooled, it is not separated.
- **The carried uncertainty is not calibrated.** It is too wide in the core and too narrow in the
  tail, and covers 0.79 across a turn.
- **Limits:**
  - windows are placed past true manoeuvres;
  - spec 028's hand-over defect is kept;
  - one simulator, contact 1.

## What it means for the owner's question

**A qualified yes.**
- Where the ownship holds course through the whole window and the target is far, the previous
  track constrains the next window far better than the window alone: about twice as well beyond
  35 km.
- It does about as well as re-solving the previous bearings, and needs only four numbers and a
  covariance.

At ordinary ranges the benefit is not shown. Where contact is lost, nothing in this design answers
well.

## Next (the owner's choice)

- **Recommended default:**
  - Adopt 2-hour `ctl-prior` with its guard as the standing chained system for the tended
    contact, up to 12 h, within the limits above.
  - Keep the joint solve as the equal alternative where the bearings are kept.
  - Close the long-engagement track with one synthesis of specs 023 to 032.
- **Alternatives:**
  - an E2 study at ordinary ranges (15–25 km, range kept while holding course);
  - repair the hand-over and the process-noise shape, then check coverage across a turn;
  - predict through lost-contact windows and score both systems there.
