What was not achieved

  • One number for "how much is identifiability": it runs from 0.35 to 0.86 with the threshold.
  • A bound: the measure is not additive, and the references are flattered by true times.
  • A sound rule: my second condition counted manoeuvres, not cost, and turns on the match tolerance.
  • Course and speed in the estimands; a joint detectability; tests for the new runner.

What comes next

  • Recommended: a manoeuvre prior that adapts to the period. Classical, CPU.
  • Registered against the control, with the true-prior search (1.17) as its ceiling.
  • Beside it: a hedged track where the limit is identifiability.
  • Not now: a GPU proposer. The evidence would veto much of what it proposed.
