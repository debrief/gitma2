## Why (diagnostic, after the fact; nothing here changes the outcome)

The registered statistic is a median over rows pooled from about 20 engagements. Each
reference fit is one parameter estimate per engagement, so its rows share one error: an
engagement contributes, in effect, one draw. Per-engagement efficiencies range from 0.19 to
3.9 on validation and from 0.17 to 13.9 on tuning, as single draws of |error| / sd would.
Resampling engagements, the statistic's 95% interval is **[0.48, 1.59]** on validation and
**[0.71, 1.63]** on tuning (point 1.28): on tuning the band's lower edge was already inside
the interval. The check could not tell a sound reference from a faulty one at this size, and
the decision record did not state its power; that is the registration's fault.

It does not show the reference is unsound either: nothing in these figures separates the
two. The reference fitted with free times and the prior (`ref-span`) reads 1.63, as on
tuning (1.59).
