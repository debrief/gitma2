## What it says, critically

1. **Across gaps of half an hour or more, little carries.**
   - Spec 025's joining gain (3.10 on Annex B, gaps 5–30 min) shrinks to 1.20, which is not separated.
   - It falls with the gap: about 1.4 at 30–50 min and 0.80 beyond 70 min.
   - None of the bands is separated (31, 20 and 23 periods).
2. **The carried prior does nothing useful here.**
   - k4 is level with `ctl` alone (median 1.00).
   - Its stated uncertainty covers the truth in every carried period (median d² 0.81): it has
     grown so wide over an hour's gap that it says almost nothing.
   - The uninflated prior harms (0.73), being both narrow and wrong after an hour of unseen
     manoeuvres. Only 40% of carried periods hold the truth inside its 95% region.
3. **Most periods answer alone in this simulator.**
   - The ownship turns every 20–40 min, so 58 of 74 later periods contain their own turn, against
     about half in spec 025's generator data.
   - The run therefore changes two things at once: the gap length, and how often a period has its
     own turn.
   - The gap-band readings hold the second roughly fixed and still show the fade. The comparison
     with spec 025's numbers does not.
4. **Cost.** Joining costs about 16 times `ctl` alone and does not pay for itself beyond about an
   hour's gap in this run.
5. One seed and 40 engagements: every interval here is wide.

## What this suggests (owner's choice)

- **An operating rule:** join periods separated by gaps up to about an hour, and solve alone
  beyond that.
  - It is suggested by the band trend, not shown: no band is separated.
  - Registering it as a decided rule would need several times these engagements per band (about
    6 CI hours a run).
- **Separating the two factors:** the same gaps with an ownship holding course for 1–2 h (spec
  029's long legs), where periods do not answer alone.
- **Stopping:** record that beyond about an hour neither joining nor carrying is shown to help,
  and keep `ctl` alone as the cheap default there.
