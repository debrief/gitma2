## What it says, critically

1. An honest uncertainty removes the harm. Where the carried prior as built harmed periods with
   their own ownship turn (0.51 on seed 361), k4 is level (0.97, 1.10). The gain where the period
   has no own turn stays (1.5 to 2.1).
2. **The seed-361 harm did not repeat on seed 360**: the uninflated prior is 1.13 own-turn and 1.62
   overall there. So the harm was partly a property of that seed; k4's case rests on removing a
   tail that appears on some seeds, not on a consistent loss of the uninflated version.
3. **k4 is crude and now over-cautious**: median d² 0.4 to 0.9 against 3.36 for a calibrated
   prior; 4 was the coarsest point of a two-point grid. A scale between 2 and 4, or one
   that depends on the previous fit's quality, was not tried.
4. **It does not replace joining on Annex B**: k4 ÷ joint is 2.1 to 2.2 on both seeds. On busier
   targets it is close (1.15 to 1.18, not separated). Joining stays standing; a carried prior is
   what is available when the earlier bearings are not kept.
5. The mixture did nothing beyond inflation: with the evidence choosing the part, it chose the
   carried part 70 to 90% of the time and did not fix coverage.
6. Seeds 360 and 361 are spent (spec 027's validation and test), and seed 361 also chose; the
   check on 360 is one seed.
