## What the figures say, and what they do not

- **Most of the gain is rows `ctl` alone cannot answer.** Rows are chosen by the bound from
  the span's bearings, so a period without its own ownship turn is scored on rows only the
  joined data determine. On rows the period determines alone, alone ÷ joint is 2.64 Annex B,
  2.21 wild bearings, 3.56 held course and **1.28 [0.95, 1.75] busier targets (not shown)**.
  Periods with no ownship turn of their own: 4.35 Annex B, 2.79 busier; periods with one:
  1.65 and 1.13 [0.90, 1.43].
- **Spread.** Median per-period ratio 2.09 Annex B, 1.69 busier; twice as bad in 12% and 15%
  of periods (4% held course). The gain is typical, not a tail, but one period in eight is
  much worse joined.
- **Gaps.** Of true manoeuvres inside gaps the joint solve declares 40 of 43 (Annex B), 83 of
  130 (busier: 36% missed), 34 of 43 (wild bearings); it declares one in 21% of quiet gaps
  (Annex B) and 15% on held course. Periods next to a gap that held a manoeuvre gain less on
  busier targets (ratio of ratios 1.73 [1.13, 2.73]) and wild bearings; on Annex B (2.67 against
  3.82) the difference is not separated.
- **What is left.** Joint ÷ the true history across the span: 1.71 Annex B, 2.03 busier
  (a reference with free times and the prior, not a bound).
- **What the gaps cost.** Every bearing ÷ joint 0.61 Annex B, 0.65 busier.
- **Not a learned result.** Nothing is trained: the joint solve is `ctl` unchanged on more
  data.
