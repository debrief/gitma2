# Spec 037: a development reading with the pilot checkpoint (not the registered model)

After the three registered fine-tunes were cut off (`../20261123-ft-runs/`), the pilot checkpoint
(spec 012's seed 1 fine-tuned for 4,000 updates; `ft-s1` in the tables) was scored on CPU on the
first 300 σ = 1° engagements of **spent seed 321** under mismatch off, the primary condition
`all-central` and the held-out condition `ho-both`, beside the unchanged seed 1 regressor
(`reg-s1`), C″, C″ with the robust final solve, the solves on the true segmentation, the IMM, the
EKF and one leg, and the hybrid at four prior weights (CI run 45131235901, code 6352735, EPYC
7763; plan `configs/headroom/rm-pilot-321.json`). Tables: `tables.md`, generated by
`scripts/research/rmis_pilot_summary.py`.

**Standing**: tuning data. One training seed of a schedule a sixth as long as the registered
one, on a seed already read in step 1 (the model was not tuned on it). The registered readings
M1–M5, Y1, Y2 are not decided by this. Validation seed 370 and test seed 371 are untouched.

## What it shows

- **Under the primary condition** the pilot model is at 0.109 in median range error against
  0.193 unchanged, 0.218 for C″ with the robust solve and 0.111 for the robust solve told the
  true manoeuvre times. Paired: C″-robust ÷ pilot 2.02 [1.80, 2.27] in range, 1.54 in course,
  1.38 in speed; unchanged ÷ pilot 1.69 [1.53, 1.87].
- **Under the held-out condition**, whose kinds training never drew, 0.107 against 0.162
  unchanged and 0.284 for C″-robust: C″-robust ÷ pilot 2.42 [2.11, 2.77]. The held-out
  condition does hurt C″ (0.099 clean to 0.284), so it is a test.
- **On clean data** unchanged ÷ pilot is 0.954 [0.902, 1.011] in range (not separated), 0.944
  [0.898, 0.991] in course and 0.949 [0.913, 0.989] in speed: a cost of about 5%, separated in
  course and speed.
- **The intervals**, at scale 1 with nothing fitted: the pilot's 95% range interval covers
  0.960, 0.940 and 0.964 on clean, primary and held-out data (unchanged: 0.950, 0.771, 0.822);
  speed 0.97 to 0.98; course 0.92 to 0.93 where it is stated (74% to 84% of rows).
- **The hybrid** is better than C″-robust at every weight tried and best at the smallest, 0.5,
  the edge of the grid: 1.47 [1.34, 1.62] clean and 1.87 [1.70, 2.06] under the primary
  condition in range, with course 1.37 and 1.53. Against the regressor alone it gives up range
  (0.91, 0.92) and, on clean data, gains course and speed (1.26, 1.20); under the primary
  condition course and speed are level. Few engagements get twice as bad (0.07, 0.04).

## What it does not show

- Three seeds, the full schedule, a fresh seed, or 1,000 engagements.
- That the model is level with a solve told the true manoeuvre times in anything but range:
  in course and speed it is at 0.71 to 0.83 of it everywhere.
- Robustness to real errors: both conditions are this simulator's, and the model knows the
  simulator's manoeuvre statistics, which C″ is not told.
- Calibration: coverage near 0.95 on 300 engagements at one scale is a reading, not a fit; the
  course interval says nothing on a quarter of rows.
- The hybrid's best weight: the grid's edge was best, so weights below 0.5 are untested.
