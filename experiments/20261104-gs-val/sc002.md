**Reproduced.** The ratios, their intervals and every settled median are identical. The
all-row medians differ in the fourth figure for every estimator, oracle and IMM included. The
cause is the median convention, not the estimates:
- 3,934 is an even count;
- `spike.json` averages the two middle values (`np.median`);
- `tma2-gs` takes the lower one (`inverted_cdf`, spec 004's one convention);
- on the odd settled count the two conventions agree exactly.

Gate S reports `tma2-gs`'s convention.
