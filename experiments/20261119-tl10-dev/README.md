## What it says

1. **Carried information moves every period** (ties with alone 0 to 3%, against about half for
   spec 027's carried start). It is information, not a start.
2. **Where a period has no ownship turn of its own, the prior does most of what joining does**:
   alone ÷ cprior 1.76 and 2.34; on busier targets it reaches the joint solve (1.03). On Annex B
   it does not (cprior ÷ joint 2.46 there).
3. **Where the period can answer for itself, the prior harms on Annex B** (0.57 [0.36, 0.90];
   own rows 0.57 [0.35, 0.90]): a confident, wrong carried state overrides the period's bearings.
4. **The carried uncertainty is overconfident.** The truth is inside the 95% ellipsoid in 43%
   (Annex B) and 50% (busier) of carried periods, against 95%; median squared distance 13 and 10
   against 3.4. Position alone is better but still short (67%, 70%). The Laplace covariance of a
   declared history is conditional on that history: when the history (a missed or invented
   manoeuvre near the period's end) is wrong, the state is wrong by more than the covariance says.
5. Pooled, the prior is not shown to help on Annex B and helps on busier targets; nowhere does it
   reach the joint solve on Annex B.

So a carried prior, as built, is not a substitute for joining on Annex B; its gain where a period
cannot answer alone is real, and its harm comes from overconfidence where it can.

## Candidate refinements (not run)

- Inflate the carried covariance by a factor chosen on tuning data to reach 95% coverage.
- A mixture prior: the carried Gaussian with weight w, the standing prior with 1 − w (a robust
  prior that lets the period's own bearings overrule a wrong carry), w from coverage on tuning.
- Carry the covariance marginalised over the previous period's candidate histories (the
  candidate set's probabilities), not conditional on the declared one.
