# Spec 037 step 1: the unchanged regressors under bearing-error mismatch (development data)

Nothing trained, no GPU. Spec 012's three regressors, exactly as trained on clean bearings
(weights' digests in `../../configs/headroom/rm-dev-321.json`), scored on the first 300 σ = 1°
engagements of **spent seed 321** under Gate H's sixteen conditions, beside C″, C″ with the
robust final solve, the robust and plain solves on the true segmentation, the IMM, the EKF and
one leg, all in one scoring run per condition (CI run 45076145136, code c3b4361, Xeon 6975P-C).
`RULE.md` was committed before the run. Tables: `tables.md` (generated by
`scripts/research/rmis_tables.py --rule` from `runs/<condition>/reading.json`; nothing typed by
hand); figures of record in `summary.json`.

**Standing**: tuning data on a spent seed. Not a result. The sixteen conditions are the same 300
engagements under different errors, so they are not sixteen independent samples.

## The rule's output

A = 3.09 (the regressors' range error under `all-central` ÷ their own with mismatch off; 0.192
against 0.062). B = C″-robust ÷ regressors = 1.179 [1.065, 1.313]. A is far above 1.10: the
regressor **does not hold up** by the rule, and step 2 (the fine-tune) proceeds.

## What the tables show

- **Clean data**: the regressors are at 0.062, level with the solve told the true manoeuvre times
  (0.062; paired 1.00 [0.89, 1.12]) and 1.70 [1.51, 1.93] ahead of C″-robust in range. In course
  and speed they are behind the true-segmentation solve (0.75, 0.71) and about level with C″.
  This repeats spec 012 on another seed.
- **Under mismatch the regressors degrade a great deal, and still lead C″ in most conditions.**
  Their error rises 3.1 times under the primary condition and 5.5 times under `all-harsh`. C″
  with the robust solve rises 2.2 and 2.8 times from a worse start. Paired, the regressors are
  ahead of C″-robust in range in 14 of 16 conditions, including the primary (1.18); under
  `out-harsh` and `all-harsh` nothing is separated (0.98, 0.91), and at far range under those two
  they are behind (0.79, 0.73).
- **Which kinds hurt the regressor**: outliers most (0.070, 0.105, 0.231 from mild to harsh),
  then correlated error (0.106 to 0.132); bias a little (0.066 to 0.093); an untold error level
  not at all (0.061 to 0.064). The solve on the true segmentation with Huber costs barely moves
  under outliers (0.063 to 0.072): against it the regressors fall to 0.59 and 0.33.
- **The room for a fine-tune**: against the robust solve told the true manoeuvre times the
  regressors are at 0.58 [0.52, 0.66] under the primary condition. That reference is told the
  manoeuvre times and is not attainable; it shows that the bearings still hold the information.
- **The intervals** (variance head at scale 1, nothing fitted): on clean data the stated 95%
  range interval covers 0.947 and speed 0.972; course covers 0.926 where it is stated (85% of
  rows; the rest are "undetermined"). Under mismatch range coverage falls with the error: 0.77
  under the primary condition, 0.63 under `all-harsh`. The model does not know its bearings are
  worse than it was told.

## Limits

- 300 engagements; a ratio within about 10% of 1 is not separated.
- The regressor was trained on this simulator's manoeuvre statistics; C″ is not told them.
- Median over engagements of an engagement's median error over five scored rows; the classical
  filters (IMM, EKF, one leg) answer about three quarters of rows and are scored on those.
- Efficiency columns divide by the bound under the true error model of each condition.
- Clean coverage near 0.95 is one seed's figure at one scale; it is not a calibration claim.
