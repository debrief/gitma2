### What the reviewers confirmed

- **Causality (pre-test audit).** Replacing every bearing and ownship sample after row k with noise
  at the same length leaves E-s's segmentation and E's estimate at k bit-identical (18 of 18 rows,
  carried weights); cutting the arrays changes estimates only in the last bits (padding length,
  a known effect). E-s's features are causal (left-padded convolutions, online ownship codes,
  previous-row innovations); E-w's inputs after k are zeroed, attention is causal and the layer
  masks rows after k. No use of truth. Red team later confirmed E-s's features exclude the zig row.
- **Statistics** recomputed every gated number from `errors.npz` with the repo's bootstrap: bit
  for bit (G2-d +0.025008 [+0.018467, +0.030531]); an independent bootstrap gives
  [0.0185, 0.0306], SD 0.0027, no resample at or below zero. Pairing (one cluster per scenario,
  4,983, shared resamples), gap recovered as a ratio of medians within each resample, and missing
  rows charged +inf for every estimator (non-finite: C 100, e-s3 99, ew-s3 102). Four criteria all
  required of one pre-selected model is an intersection-union test (no multiplicity correction).
  Stable: +0.020 to +0.024 at each G5 time point, +0.020 to +0.031 in each 1,000-ordinal block,
  leave-one-scenario-out moves the point by at most 3e-4; E is better than C on 56% of rows.
  Validation numbers and the carried rule (median of 0.9095, 0.9181, 0.9207: e-s3) recomputed.
- **Reproducibility.** Plan digest `6092c364…` = decision; C's code `4d2c084f…` and parameters
  `60e6ee63…` at every commit used (`src/` unchanged from the decision commit 36bfab8 to the test
  scoring commit 237c87b); carried weights `a5485318…` = SHA-256 of `2b3b9c73…+58b51d10…`; seed 319
  regenerated byte for byte at 4a0cc63 (all four splits); 4,983 σ = 1° test scenarios; order:
  decision 07:45 → `carried.json` 17:57 → seed 319 and test plans 17:59–18:01 → results 19:19,
  19:41, 20:56 → score 22:56. Local re-runs of whole CI chunks are byte-identical (ew-s3; e-s3 on
  one chunk) or differ by ≤ 3.6e-7 relative on another CPU family, with no flipped decision. Every
  segmentation and prediction file covers all 4,983 ordinals once.
- **Numerics.** Decision tables accept exactly when the margin is positive (14,351 and 282,429
  decisions); zero evidence gives C's loss (52.3123); `c_mismatch_rows` 0; validation's hard rule

### Findings and responses

**R2-1 [minor-to-major, red team N1] The controls' README said validation agreed in direction for
E-w − fade** (validation: −0.0025 [−0.0085, +0.0073]). *Fixed* (968837d): the attribution to E-w

**R2-2 [minor, red team N2–N4; statistics N-a, N-b] Wording.** No equivalence claim ("E-s adds
nothing shown beyond the zig veto", not "is a zig veto"); the rules' settings came from the
learned models, so E − C″ is conservative and the rules' 72% is an upper bound on what a designer

after it); the write-up uses the red team's accepted wording.

**R2-3 [minor, geometry] The veto covers only the end of the first ownship turn** (`zig_index` is
the first row after the turn; turn starts a median 8 rows earlier, up to 33; the second turn,

overstated for E; no reading changes. **R2-4 [minor, geometry]** the look-ahead caveat overstated
the control's advantage (at most 6 rows, and only once the decision row reaches zig − 6). *Both
stated* in the controls' README.

**R2-5 [minor, numerics N1] Fade and E-w rescale the bearing weights**, so their covariances are not
on C's scale. No scored point estimate is affected; any later covariance gating or efficiency
claim for them needs recalibration. *Recorded* as a limit.

**R2-6 [minor, numerics N2; reproducibility R2-1; statistics] CPU-family dependence.** The scorer
re-solves C, one leg, the oracle and GLR + confirm: between the gate run (AMD EPYC 9V45) and the

1.4e-5. *Stated* in the record's README, the controls' README and AGENTS.md; follow-on: store C's
scored errors rather than re-solving them.

**R2-7 [minor, reproducibility R2-2] Control prediction headers** carry the empty-string weights
digest and not the window or fade; both are bound through `plan_sha256`. *Follow-on* (cosmetic).

**Still open as follow-ons (agreed in round 1):** G7 (time-late variant), G9–G12 (shuffled-rule
