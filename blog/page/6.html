Why the time-late control is twice the reference on busier targets: part hidden manoeuvres, part the wrong prior

November 14, 2026 · 6 min read

Study L left one gap open on both sides: on targets that manoeuvre more often and less sharply than Annex B says, the classical control is about twice a fit told the true history. The owner asked for one cheap classical study of whether that gap is identifiability, before anyone considers a learned proposer. It is done, on a fresh seed, with nothing trained. The answer is not one number. In about two thirds of periods the limit is manoeuvres the bearings barely show; in under a third the control misses manoeuvres the bearings do show, and at least half of those misses are the prior's, not the search's.
