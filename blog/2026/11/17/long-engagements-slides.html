Slides: longer engagements, 6 to 48 hours

November 17, 2026 · 3 min read

Issue #42 asked what it would take to run our TMA on engagements of 6 to 48 hours. On four simulated engagements (seed 348), every standing system ran to the end, on windows of the size it was built for. The time-late control keeps its accuracy at every length. Running it on a whole long period does not work as written, and several parts of the code assume one ownship zig. An investigation, not a gate. Spec: specs/023-long-engagements/.

What was built​

  • A long-engagement simulator (src/tma2/long/sim.py), separate from generator 0.6.0, whose output stays byte-identical. The target follows Annex B's statistics. The ownship turns at the start of each 20–40 minute leg and keeps the target at 3–25 km, steering on the true target position.
  • Windows (src/tma2/long/windows.py) in the form every estimator already reads, and C″'s zig veto applied at every ownship turn.
  • Real-time and time-late runners (src/tma2/long/realtime.py, timelate.py), the tma2-long CLI and the long.yml workflow.
  • Long-engagement sections on the viewer and the time-late page, precomputed in Python.
  • Research note 12 (docs/research/12-long-engagements.md) and the record experiments/20261117-long/ (tables.md generated from summary.json).

What was found​

real-time IMM (answered)19.0% (1.00)35.2% (1.00)15.0% (0.96)30.9% (0.81)
  • ctl on one long period: 1 min for half an hour, 114 min for 6 hours, and at most ten manoeuvres (6 hours held 16).
  • The real-time model re-solves its 2-hour window for every estimate: 27–37 s on one core.
  • The IMM over the whole engagement took 426 s at 48 hours against 6 s at 6; the cause is not established.
  • The Cramér–Rao bound over a whole engagement needs 4.5 GB at 48 hours.

What was not achieved​

  • One engagement per duration: these are anecdotes, not rates.
  • The scenario is kind: the ownship steers on the truth, contact is never lost, one target.
  • No whole-engagement solve, and E does not run in the browser on these engagements.

What comes next (the owner's choice)​

Recommended: carry state between windows (each window starts from the previous solution), then an incremental C so a real-time estimate costs the same at hour 48 as at hour 2; realism (an ownship steering on its own solution, contact gaps, a second target) before any registered study. The next fresh seed is 349.
